# Brand assets

The MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the Graphite UI source code and
documentation. It does not cover the brand assets listed here. These belong to
Simorka Designs, and all rights in them are reserved.

## What is excluded

- The Simorka Designs name, brand identity and logo
- The SD System mark (`public/graphite/sd-system-logo.png`)
- The Graphite UI name and logo (`public/graphite/mark.png`, the favicons and
  touch icon in `public/`, and the mark drawn in `components/brand.tsx`)

## What that means

You may use, copy, change and ship the code under the MIT licence, including in
commercial work. You may not use the names or marks above to brand your own
product, or in a way that suggests Simorka Designs made or endorses it.

If you fork or redistribute the project, replace these assets with your own.

Saying that your work is built with Graphite UI, or linking to this repository,
is fine.

## Colors

The default source color and the palette the site ships with are the Simorka
Designs brand colors. The color values themselves are not restricted. Graphite
is built to take your own source color, so please set one rather than shipping
the default as your brand.
